Privacy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This notice covers the threadwitness.com website. How the Thread Witness product handles data, including posts by people in the sources we read, is described on How we source data.

What this website does not do

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics.
  • No trackers, pixels or fingerprinting.
  • No requests to third-party sites: fonts, scripts and images are served from this site.

Hosting

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical request data, such as IP addresses, for security and operations. We do not receive those records.

Email

If you write to hello@threadwitness.com, we use your message and address to reply. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your address. Mail to this address passes through our email forwarding provider and our mailbox provider.

Contact

Questions about this notice: hello@threadwitness.com

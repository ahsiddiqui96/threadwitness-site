Pricing

Early access. Reading public sources runs today. Classification, extraction, merging, scoring, report writing and the verifier are built and in testing. Alerts and ticket sync are built and being prepared for production, and the dashboard is in development. Prices and inclusions below are confirmed in writing at quote.

What early-access customers receive

  • The report, in HTML and PDF
  • The evidence file, in CSV and JSON
  • The readout and written answers included in the plan
  • Delivery by email

Lines marked “when the dashboard opens” or “in development” below are not yet available. The audit is how early-access customers start today.

How we price

You pay per tracked product. We do not charge per mention, so a busy product does not cost more to track. Viewers will be unlimited and free on every plan, because a report is meant to be forwarded; editors, the people who set statuses, create tickets or change alert rules, are capped per plan. Annual plans are billed in advance by invoice or purchase order.
